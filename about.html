About Bonkey Math

One race track. Eleven categories. Built with love, Explorer.

What is Bonkey Math?

Bonkey Math is an offline, privacy-first flash-card race for kids and families — covering everything from Pre-K counting to college-entry Trig & Pre-Calculus, in eleven categories across three tiers each.

Race your own personal-best time solo, practice untimed with no pressure, or connect two devices over Bluetooth for a real-time head-to-head race. Every mode shares the same seeded, on-device problem generator, so practice always carries forward.

Bonkey Math is fully offline, contains no advertising, and makes no network calls during gameplay. All progress is stored locally on your device. It's safe for the whole family.

Meet the mascot

Say hi to Bonkey!

Bonkey is a hyperactive, always-smiling rabbit who loves racing, space, and solving problems at warp speed. Bonkey shows up as the racer on the home screen and as a cheerful sidekick during gameplay to celebrate wins.

Bonkey's look is classic space explorer: a cream plush bunny in a space-print t-shirt covered in planets and stars, teal shorts, and a small orange backpack.

No purchase ever required to play

Privacy first

We collect no personal data. No analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no trackers. All progress stays on your device. Read our full privacy policy.

No chance-based rewards

No loot boxes, no gacha, no randomized unlocks — progress is always predictable and skill-based, in line with children's-app best practice.
