Privacy Policy

Last updated July 8, 2026

Bonkey Math does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers of any kind. Every math problem is generated on the device you're playing on, and every piece of your progress stays on that device unless you choose to play a Bluetooth race, in which case only the minimum data needed to run that race is shared directly with the other player's device — never with us or with anyone else.

Who we are

Bonkey Math is made by Bonkey Apps. If you have any questions about this policy or about the app, contact us at bonkey.apps@gmail.com.

Information we collect

None. Specifically, Bonkey Math does not:

  • Require or offer account creation, sign-in, or sign-up of any kind.
  • Collect names, email addresses, birthdates, or any other personal information.
  • Include any analytics software development kit (SDK).
  • Include any advertising SDK or show any ads.
  • Use cookies, device fingerprinting, advertising identifiers, or any other cross-app or cross-site tracking technology.
  • Transmit gameplay data, problem content, or scores to any server. There is no server — Bonkey Math works entirely offline.

What stays on your device

Progress, personal-best times, settings, and purchase-entitlement records are stored locally on your device only. Uninstalling the app removes this data. We have no access to it, cannot see it, and never receive a copy of it.

Bluetooth head-to-head races

When two players race head-to-head, their devices connect directly to each other over Bluetooth — not through the internet and not through any Bonkey Apps server. This local connection is used only to:

  • Agree on a shared random seed so both devices generate the identical set of math problems.
  • Synchronize the race start ("3-2-1-Go").
  • Exchange lightweight progress check-ins (how many problems solved, and for older-tier players, a streak count) so each player can see how the race is going.
  • Share final results once both players finish.

The actual math problems and each player's individual answers are never transmitted — only the progress markers above. Nothing from a Bluetooth race is sent to Bonkey Apps or stored anywhere beyond the two devices playing.

Purchases

Bonkey Math offers a one-time purchase to unlock the full set of categories and tiers; there is no subscription. A family-plan variant covers multiple children under one purchase. Purchases are processed entirely by the app store you downloaded from (Apple App Store, Google Play, or the Amazon Appstore) — Bonkey Apps does not receive or store your payment details. Every purchase flow requires a parental-gate step before any transaction can complete, so a purchase can't happen by a child tapping through gameplay alone.

Children's privacy

Bonkey Math is designed for a general audience that includes children, and this policy is written with that in mind. Because we collect no personal information from any user, regardless of age, there is no personal information collected from children to disclose, use, or share. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the app contains no chat, no social features, and no way for a child to contact anyone through the app other than the direct local Bluetooth link used for a race the child's own device initiates or accepts.

Data sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or otherwise disclose any data to third parties, because we do not collect any data to share in the first place.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "Last updated" date above.

Contact us

Questions about privacy or this app? Email bonkey.apps@gmail.com.
