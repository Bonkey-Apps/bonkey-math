Eleven rungs, one race track

Every category is built from procedurally generated problems, not a fixed worksheet, so there's always a fresh race to run. Tiers are freely selectable within an unlocked category — no grinding required to reach the level a player is ready for.

  1. Pre-K–K

    Counting & Number Sense

    First numbers, comparing, and one-to-one counting.

  2. K–2nd grade

    Addition

    Single-digit sums up through triple-digit addition with carrying.

  3. 1st–3rd grade

    Subtraction

    From single-digit facts to borrowing and negative results.

  4. 2nd–4th grade

    Multiplication

    Times tables 1–12, then two-digit multiplication.

  5. 3rd–5th grade

    Division

    Basic facts, remainders, and long division with two-digit divisors.

  6. 5th–7th grade

    Higher Math

    Fractions, percentages, exponents, roots, and order of operations (PEMDAS).

  7. 3rd–6th grade

    Geometry & Measurement

    Shapes, angles, area, and units.

  8. 6th–8th grade

    Statistics & Probability

    Averages, data, and chance.

  9. 8th–10th grade

    Advanced Algebra

    Solving for the unknown, including negative exponents.

  10. 11th grade–college entry

    Trig & Pre-Calculus

    The on-ramp to calculus.

  11. K–8th grade

    Word Problems

    Reading comprehension meets arithmetic, single-step to multi-step.

One engine, every way to play

Solo and head-to-head races share the same seeded, on-device problem generator — so practice today carries straight into race night.

Solo race

Race your own personal-best time for any category and tier.

Practice mode

Untimed, no-pressure practice for building real skill before the clock starts.

Bluetooth head-to-head

Two devices exchange a shared seed and race the identical problem set — no internet, no server, and problem content never leaves the device.

Mini-games

Bite-sized challenge modes layered on the same category and tier content.
