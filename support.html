Support

Frequently asked questions for Bonkey Math

Does the app require an internet connection?

No. Bonkey Math is fully offline — every problem is generated on your device, and solo play never needs a network connection. Bluetooth head-to-head races connect two nearby devices directly to each other, not through the internet.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the app, go to Settings → Restore Purchases, and sign in with the same Apple, Google, or Amazon account you used originally.

What's included for free?

Addition and Subtraction (Tiers 1–2) are free to play, untimed practice included. No purchase is ever required to try the app.

What does the full unlock include?

A single one-time purchase unlocks every category and tier — Multiplication, Division, Higher Math, Geometry & Measurement, Statistics & Probability, Advanced Algebra, Trig & Pre-Calculus, Word Problems, plus Tier 3 of Addition and Subtraction. There is no subscription; a family-plan option covers multiple children under one purchase.

How does Bluetooth head-to-head work?

Two devices exchange a shared random seed so both generate the exact same sequence of problems, then start together on a synced countdown. Each device tracks its own timer and score locally; only lightweight progress check-ins are exchanged during the race. Problem content and individual answers are never transmitted.

Is there real-money gambling, loot boxes, or chance-based rewards?

No. Bonkey Math has no chance-based reward mechanics of any kind. Progress and unlocks are always predictable and skill-based.
